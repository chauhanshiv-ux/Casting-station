Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how The Casting Station handles personal information shared with us through casting enquiries, audition submissions, applications, social media, advertisements and other communications.

1. About this policy

The Casting Station (“we”, “us” or “our”) is a casting and talent-related service. We may receive information from actors, performers, talent, applicants, their representatives and people who contact us about professional opportunities.

This policy applies to information you choose to share with us when you respond to an advertisement on Meta platforms such as Facebook or Instagram, complete a lead form, contact us through social media or messaging services, send us an email, or submit material for a casting opportunity.

2. Information we collect

Depending on the opportunity and how you contact us, information you provide may include:

  • Contact details such as your name, phone number, email address and city or location.
  • Casting and professional details such as age or age range, languages, skills, experience and availability.
  • Photographs, headshots, portfolios, resumes, showreels, audition recordings, self-tapes and other performance material.
  • Information contained in application forms, messages, emails and conversations with us.
  • Other details you choose to provide, or that are reasonably required for a particular casting brief.

Please share only information relevant to the opportunity. Do not send sensitive personal information unless it is specifically requested for a legitimate project-related reason.

3. How we use information

We may use the information you submit for casting and related professional purposes, including to:

  • Review profiles and assess suitability for casting briefs, auditions and projects.
  • Contact you about auditions, callbacks, casting updates or relevant opportunities.
  • Prepare and share relevant talent profiles or audition material with authorised people involved in a project.
  • Manage applications, maintain casting records and respond to enquiries.
  • Operate, administer and improve our casting services and communications.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations and address disputes, fraud or misuse.

Submitting your profile does not guarantee an audition, selection, role, employment or any other engagement.

4. When information is shared

Casting work may require us to share relevant information with people involved in a project. Depending on the opportunity, this may include production houses, producers, directors, casting teams, agencies or other authorised project representatives.

We aim to share only information relevant to the casting requirement. We do not publish your personal contact details or audition material publicly as a routine part of the casting process.

We may also use service providers that help us operate forms, communications, file storage or other business systems. They may access information only as needed to provide those services. We may disclose information where required by law or where necessary to protect legal rights or safety.

5. Storage and security

Information may be held in email accounts, digital forms, devices, cloud storage or other tools used to manage casting work. We take reasonable administrative and technical steps intended to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. While we take reasonable care, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. How long we keep information

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the casting, project, communication or record-keeping purpose for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law. Retention periods may vary depending on the nature of the project and the material submitted.

7. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to request access to information you have submitted, correction of inaccurate details, an update to your contact information, or deletion of information where applicable.

To help us locate your records, contact us through the official communication channel you used to submit your profile. Include enough information to identify the relevant submission. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Some information may need to be retained where it is required for an ongoing project, legitimate record-keeping or compliance with applicable law.

8. Children and minors

If a casting opportunity involves a person under 18, a parent or legal guardian should submit or supervise the submission and provide any required permission. We may request confirmation of a parent’s or guardian’s authority before proceeding. Please do not submit a minor’s information without appropriate parent or guardian involvement.

9. Third-party services and links

We may use third-party platforms and services, including social media, online forms, messaging tools and cloud storage, to receive or manage information. Those services may process information under their own privacy policies and terms. We encourage you to review the policies of any third-party service you use.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, services or applicable requirements. The latest version will be published on this page, with the revised date shown at the top.

11. Contact us

For questions, concerns or requests relating to this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact The Casting Station through the official communication channel you used to reach us, such as our official social media account or the channel through which you submitted your information.

When contacting us, please provide enough details for us to identify your enquiry or submission. Please do not include passwords or unnecessary sensitive information in your message.

This page describes general information-handling practices. Before publishing, make sure the policy accurately reflects the tools you use, who can access submitted profiles and audition material, and how long you retain them.
